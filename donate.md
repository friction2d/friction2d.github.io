# Support Friction

Friction is, and always will be, free and open-source. If you use it and want to help ensure its continued development, consider supporting us!

**Your donations go directly towards development and maintenance.**

## How to Donate

Currently, the best way to support us financially is through GitHub Sponsors. You can choose a one-time tip or a monthly tier that works for you.

    Support us on GitHub Sponsors

</p>

*More options will be added.*

## Other ways to help

Not in a position to donate? No problem at all! You can still make a huge impact by:

- Contributing code or helping improve our documentation.
- Reporting bugs and feedback in our issue tracker.
- Spreading the word by sharing Friction with others.

Thank you for using Friction and being part of our community!
